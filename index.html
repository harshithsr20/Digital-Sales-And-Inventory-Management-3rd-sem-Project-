Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to sign in every day?

No. DukaanHub maintains verified persistent sessions so you land immediately on your counter terminal.

How does it prevent stock overselling?

Stock is checked before invoice confirmation. If an SKU balance is insufficient, the system alerts and halts entry.

Are GST rates calculated automatically?

Yes. It automatically calculates taxable subtotals and splits CGST, SGST, and IGST according to HSN tax rules.

Can I handle customer returns and debit notes?

Yes. Returns generate clean credit notes that update both ledger balances and net sales figures instantly.
